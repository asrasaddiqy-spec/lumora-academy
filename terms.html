Terms & Conditions

Guidelines for learning and participating at Lumora Academy.

1. Introduction

These Terms & Conditions describe the general guidelines for using the Lumora Academy website and participating in its educational programs.

2. Registration

Students should provide accurate and complete information when submitting a registration form. Registration does not automatically guarantee enrollment in a program.

3. Eligibility

Our educational programs are intended for girls and young women aged 15 to 25. Students under 18 are encouraged to involve a parent or guardian in the registration process.

4. Learning Environment

Students are expected to contribute to a respectful, supportive, and inclusive online learning environment. Discrimination, harassment, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

5. Attendance and Participation

Students are encouraged to attend scheduled classes, participate in learning activities, and communicate respectfully with instructors.

6. Learning Materials

Educational materials provided by Lumora Academy should be used for personal learning purposes. Students should not redistribute or publish these materials without permission.

7. Communication

Students should use the academy's designated communication channels for questions, feedback, and academic support.

8. Program Changes

Lumora Academy may make reasonable changes to course schedules, learning activities, or program arrangements. Students will be informed of relevant changes.

9. Privacy

Personal information submitted through our website and registration forms is handled according to our Privacy Policy.

10. Updates to These Terms

These Terms & Conditions may be updated as Lumora Academy develops. Updated terms will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about these terms, please contact Lumora Academy through our Contact page.
