Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

1. Introduction

Lumora Academy respects the privacy of its students and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you interact with our website and educational programs.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through our registration and contact forms, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Age
  • Selected educational program
  • English language level
  • Messages and inquiries

3. How We Use Information

We use submitted information to review registrations, respond to inquiries, communicate about educational programs, and improve our learning services.

4. Information Protection

We take reasonable steps to keep submitted information private and limit access to authorized individuals who need it for academy-related responsibilities.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell students' personal information. Our forms and registration records may be processed through third-party services such as Google Forms and Google Sheets.

6. Children's Privacy

Our programs may be available to learners aged 15 and above. We encourage learners under 18 to involve a parent or guardian when registering. We aim to handle younger learners' information with particular care.

7. Your Choices

You may contact Lumora Academy to ask questions about information you have submitted or request corrections where appropriate.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website and educational services develop. Any revised version will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
